# Orbita Cloud account FAQ

## Resetting your password

Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page. The reset link is valid for 60 minutes. If you use single sign-on (SSO), reset the password with your identity provider instead.

## Changing your email address

Change the login email in Settings → Profile. Orbita Cloud sends a confirmation link to the new address, and the change takes effect only after you click it.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) can be turned on in Settings → Security with any authenticator app. If you lose your device, use one of the ten backup codes shown when you enabled 2FA.

## Seats and team members

Team plans include 10 seats. Owners invite members from Settings → Team. Extra seats cost $15 per month each.

## Locked accounts

After 10 failed sign-in attempts an account is locked for 30 minutes. Support can unlock it sooner after verifying the account owner.

## Deleting your account

Owners can delete the workspace from Settings → Danger zone. Data is kept for 30 days and then removed permanently.
