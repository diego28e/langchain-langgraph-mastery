# Orbita Cloud billing FAQ

Orbita Cloud is a fictional SaaS used in this course. It stores and charts data from Orbita Labs weather stations.

## Plans and prices

Orbita Cloud has three plans. Starter costs $19 per month and includes 3 stations. Pro costs $49 per month and includes 25 stations. Team costs $199 per month and includes unlimited stations and 10 seats. Annual billing gives two months free.

## When you are charged

Plans renew on the same day each month. Invoices are numbered like INV-1001 and are emailed to the billing contact. Upgrades are charged right away, prorated to the end of the current period. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal.

## Refund policy

You can ask for a refund within 30 days of a charge. Refunds go back to the original card in 5 to 10 business days. Refunds above $50 need approval from a billing manager before they are issued. Duplicate charges are always refunded in full.

## Failed payments

If a card payment fails, Orbita Cloud retries it after 3 days and again after 7 days. After the third failure the account becomes read-only until the card is updated in Settings → Billing.

## Taxes and receipts

VAT or sales tax is added according to the billing address. You can download PDF receipts from Settings → Billing → Invoices.
