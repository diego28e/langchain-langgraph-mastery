# FAQ: Signing in to the Orbita app

## I am locked out
After **5 failed sign-in attempts** the account is locked for **15 minutes**. Wait, then use "Forgot password".

## Two-factor authentication
Two-factor authentication is optional for home accounts and **mandatory for Business plans**. Use any authenticator app.

## Can several people share a station?
Yes. Invite family members from **Settings > Sharing**. Viewers can read data but can't change settings.
