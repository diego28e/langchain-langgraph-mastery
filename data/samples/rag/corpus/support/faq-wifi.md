# FAQ: Wi-Fi connection problems

## Which networks work?
The display bridge only joins **2.4 GHz** Wi-Fi networks. 5 GHz-only networks are not supported. Networks with a captive portal (hotels, some offices) do not work.

## How do I reset the Wi-Fi settings?
Hold the bridge's pairing button for **10 seconds** until the light blinks orange, then run "Add bridge" again in the Orbita app. Your readings are kept.

## The bridge keeps dropping off
Move it within 10 m of the router, away from microwaves. If it still drops, assign it a fixed IP address in your router.
