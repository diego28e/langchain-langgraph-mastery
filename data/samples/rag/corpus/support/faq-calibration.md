# FAQ: Calibration

## My pressure reading is different from the airport's
Airports report pressure adjusted to sea level. Set your altitude in **Settings > Sensors > Altitude** and the app shows sea-level pressure too.

## How often should I recalibrate humidity?
Humidity sensors drift slowly. Recalibrate every **12 months** with the salt-test kit included with Pro, Marine and Agro stations.

## Temperature reads high in the afternoon
The station is probably in direct sun or near a wall. Read the installation guide: mount it in the shade, at least 1 m from walls.
