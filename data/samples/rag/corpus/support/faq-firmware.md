# FAQ: Firmware updates

## When do updates install?
Stations update **over the air** between **02:00 and 04:00 local time**, when a new version is available and the battery is above 30%.

## Can I update manually?
Yes: in the app, open the station, tap **Firmware**, then **Check now**. Don't power off the bridge during an update.

## Which version do I have?
The version is on the Firmware screen. The Nimbus Mini stays on the 3.x line; all other models receive 4.x.
