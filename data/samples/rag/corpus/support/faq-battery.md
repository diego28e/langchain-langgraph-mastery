# FAQ: Battery life

## Why does my battery drain fast in winter?
Below **-20 °C** lithium cells deliver less energy: expect about **40% shorter** battery life in long cold spells. Above 50 °C the pack protects itself and may pause charging.

## How do I check the battery level?
In the app, open the station and tap **Health**. A level under 15% triggers a push notification.

## Can I use other batteries?
Use only Orbita packs in the S, Pro, Marine and Agro models. The Mini takes standard AA alkaline or lithium cells.
