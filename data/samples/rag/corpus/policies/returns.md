# Returns policy

You can return any Orbita product within **30 days** of delivery for a full refund if it is unused and in its original box.

Opened products can also be returned within 30 days, minus a **10% restocking fee**. Stations that were mounted outdoors must be cleaned before shipping back.

To start a return, write to support with your order number. We email a prepaid label for orders shipped inside Colombia; international customers pay return shipping.
