# Installation guide

## Where to put the station
Mount the station **1.5 m above the ground**, in the shade, at least **1 m from walls** and 10 m from trees. Rain gauges need open sky above them.

## Pairing
Insert the battery, then press the bridge's pairing button once. Firmware 3.0 and later pair without a code.

## First readings
Readings appear in the app within 2 minutes. Pressure needs your altitude (see the calibration FAQ).
