# Nimbus S1

The Nimbus S1 is an Orbita Labs weather station for most home gardens.

## Specifications

- **Price:** US$149 (station and display bridge).
- **Sensors:** temperature, humidity and barometric pressure.
- **Power:** a 2,000 mAh rechargeable pack that lasts about 14 months with the solar cap.
- **Radio range:** up to 120 m line of sight between the station and the bridge.
- **Enclosure rating:** IP65.
- **Warranty:** 2 years from the delivery date.

## Who should buy it

Choose the Nimbus S1 for most home gardens. Compare all models on the products page before you buy; the warranty and the radio range are the most common reasons people pick a bigger model.
