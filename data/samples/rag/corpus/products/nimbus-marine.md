# Nimbus Marine

The Nimbus Marine is an Orbita Labs weather station for boats, docks and coastal homes.

## Specifications

- **Price:** US$429 (station and display bridge).
- **Sensors:** the Pro sensors plus a salt-spray detector.
- **Power:** a 3,400 mAh pack with a sealed solar panel.
- **Radio range:** up to 500 m line of sight between the station and the bridge.
- **Enclosure rating:** IP67.
- **Warranty:** 3 years from the delivery date.

## Who should buy it

Choose the Nimbus Marine for boats, docks and coastal homes. Compare all models on the products page before you buy; the warranty and the radio range are the most common reasons people pick a bigger model.
