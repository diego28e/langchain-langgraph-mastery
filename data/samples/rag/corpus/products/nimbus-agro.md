# Nimbus Agro

The Nimbus Agro is an Orbita Labs weather station for fields and greenhouses (it uses LoRa radio instead of Wi-Fi).

## Specifications

- **Price:** US$389 (station and display bridge).
- **Sensors:** the Pro sensors plus up to four soil-moisture probes.
- **Power:** a 5,000 mAh pack with a solar panel.
- **Radio range:** up to 2000 m line of sight between the station and the bridge.
- **Enclosure rating:** IP66.
- **Warranty:** 3 years from the delivery date.

## Who should buy it

Choose the Nimbus Agro for fields and greenhouses (it uses LoRa radio instead of Wi-Fi). Compare all models on the products page before you buy; the warranty and the radio range are the most common reasons people pick a bigger model.
