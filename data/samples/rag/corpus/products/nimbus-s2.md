# Nimbus S2

The Nimbus S2 is an Orbita Labs weather station for open gardens and rooftops.

## Specifications

- **Price:** US$199 (station and display bridge).
- **Sensors:** temperature, humidity, pressure and wind speed.
- **Power:** the same 2,000 mAh pack as the S1, about 12 months because of the anemometer.
- **Radio range:** up to 150 m line of sight between the station and the bridge.
- **Enclosure rating:** IP65.
- **Warranty:** 2 years from the delivery date.

## Who should buy it

Choose the Nimbus S2 for open gardens and rooftops. Compare all models on the products page before you buy; the warranty and the radio range are the most common reasons people pick a bigger model.
