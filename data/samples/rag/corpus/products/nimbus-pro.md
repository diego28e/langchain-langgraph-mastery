# Nimbus Pro

The Nimbus Pro is an Orbita Labs weather station for farms, schools and serious hobbyists.

## Specifications

- **Price:** US$349 (station and display bridge).
- **Sensors:** temperature, humidity, pressure, wind speed and direction, rainfall and UV index.
- **Power:** a 3,400 mAh pack with a solar panel; it runs indefinitely in daylight.
- **Radio range:** up to 300 m line of sight between the station and the bridge.
- **Enclosure rating:** IP66.
- **Warranty:** 3 years from the delivery date.
