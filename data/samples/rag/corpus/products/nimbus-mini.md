# Nimbus Mini

The Nimbus Mini is an Orbita Labs weather station for a balcony or a small garden.

## Specifications

- **Price:** US$89 (station and display bridge).
- **Sensors:** temperature and humidity.
- **Power:** two AA batteries that last about 9 months.
- **Radio range:** up to 60 m line of sight between the station and the bridge.
- **Enclosure rating:** IP54.
- **Warranty:** 1 year from the delivery date.

## Who should buy it

Choose the Nimbus Mini for a balcony or a small garden. Compare all models on the products page before you buy; the warranty and the radio range are the most common reasons people pick a bigger model.
